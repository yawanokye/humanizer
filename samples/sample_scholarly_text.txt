1.1 Background to the Study

It is important to note that digital higher education plays a crucial role in expanding participation. Moreover, the study examines various factors that shape student engagement. Moreover, the study considers access to devices, the stability of internet connectivity and the quality of learner support. Moreover, the study aims to contribute to the understanding of online learning in the present context.

The evidence indicates that participation cannot be explained by access alone. Students may possess suitable devices but still disengage when course expectations are unclear, feedback is delayed or learning activities are poorly sequenced (Adam, 2024). This distinction matters because infrastructure and pedagogy operate together rather than as independent conditions.
